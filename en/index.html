Current project · In development

Tower Defense

A tower defense RTS set in a forest. Gather and build during the day, defend the village at night.

Unreal Engine 5.5·C++·Tower Defense RTS
Project overview
Day

Building the base

Wood and stone gathering, free building placement. Villagers gather, drop off resources and build autonomously.

Exploration

Unlocking zones

The map is divided into locked zones. An unlock tower opens a new buildable zone.

Night

Defending the village

Enemies attack at night. Controllable allied troops and towers are used to defend the base.
