About This Project

Shards of Ylena is a solo action-adventure game prototype in which the player takes on the role of an agile archer. The development has focused on building core player gameplay, including fluid movement mechanics and a responsive archery system at the heart of the experience.

This prototype explores the possibilities of ranged combat in a third-person perspective, with special attention to the feel of movement and the satisfaction of landing accurate shots. The fantasy setting provides a rich backdrop for future development.
